Privacy Policy

Puzzle Walks View

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Puzzle Walks View. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile game.

By using Puzzle Walks View, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Puzzle Walks View is designed to save game information locally on your device. Depending on the features you use, the game may store:

  • Completed levels and unlocked stages.
  • Collected virtual coins, achievements, and rewards.
  • Individual scores, completion times, and game statistics.
  • Saved game progress and puzzle results.
  • Images generated from completed puzzles or game results, when this feature is used.

The game does not require an online account to save your progress. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the game.

3. How Information Is Used

Locally stored game information is used to:

  • Save and restore your game progress.
  • Track completed puzzles and unlocked levels.
  • Manage virtual coins, achievements, and rewards.
  • Calculate completion times and individual scores.
  • Display your personal ranking and game statistics.
  • Generate and display images of your game results.
  • Support the normal operation of the game.

4. Game Progress and Local Storage

Puzzle Walks View stores game information locally on the player's device. This information may include completed levels, unlocked stages, collected virtual coins, achievements, individual scores, completion times, and saved progress.

The game does not use cloud storage or online synchronization to save this progress. Game information remains on the device.

Important: Uninstalling the application, clearing its data, or resetting the device may permanently delete locally stored progress and game records.

5. Virtual Coins and Achievements

The game may include virtual coins, rewards, achievements, and progression systems. These features are provided for entertainment purposes only. Virtual coins have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money.

6. Individual Ranking

Puzzle Walks View provides an individual ranking system stored locally on the player's device.

Scores, completion times, and game records are used only to track the player's personal performance and progress. This ranking is not public, is not shared with other players, and is not transmitted to external ranking servers.

7. Generated Images

Puzzle Walks View may allow players to generate images based on completed puzzles or game results. These images are intended to display or share the player's achievements.

The game does not automatically publish these images. If you choose to share an image, the sharing process is handled through the device's available features and the service you select.

8. Third-Party Services

The game is designed to store progress and individual ranking information locally and does not require cloud synchronization or an online ranking service.

If third-party services, such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other external services, are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices and applicable providers.

9. Data Security

Game information is stored using the device's local storage mechanisms. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and using the security features provided by your device manufacturer.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Puzzle Walks View is an entertainment game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us inappropriately, please contact us using the email address below so that we can review the matter.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and ranking records remain in the application's local storage until they are deleted by the user, the application data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled.

Because this information is stored locally and is not sent to our servers, we generally cannot recover locally deleted game progress.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights regarding personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion. For privacy questions or requests, contact us by email.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the game, its features, or applicable legal requirements. Updates will be published on this page together with a revised update date.
